What the labels mean
Fit score
From 0 to 100: an AI estimate of how well the job matches your profile. 80 or more is a strong match, 65 or more a good one. Open a job to see the reasons.
Tracks
The groups of roles in your criteria. “Best match” lists your most important track first, then the highest fit score.
Visa labels
What the posting and public records say about sponsorship, H-1B approvals and E-Verify. They are for your information only and never change which jobs you see or their order.
Approve
Builds a tailored resume and answers for that job. You check the packet under Packets before anything is sent.
Snooze
Hides the job for 7 days, then it comes back to this list.
